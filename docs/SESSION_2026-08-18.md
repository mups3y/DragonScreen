## 4. FIX 2 — booster coast roll: ROOT-CAUSED, disturbance-reduction mitigation SHIPPED
Isolated from the recorder: the excursion is **triggered by the ~180° reorientation to retrograde at
coast start** (met 2137: target pitch → −178° added on top of a standing ~78° yaw slew), at 58 km where
q is negligible — so NOT aero and NOT the roll reference. **Proof of mechanism:** during the pure-yaw
slew (met 2125→2137) roll stays ~0; the instant the pitch flip is commanded on top of the yaw error,
roll explodes (tqR 0.1→6.7, omR→−5). A combined pitch+yaw slew through the per-axis control law
(`AttitudeController.DriveInner`, independent per-axis rates, lines 342-372) does not compose into a
clean rotation and bleeds into roll, which the ~10×-weak roll channel (34 vs 329/367 kN·m, physical)
can't reject → overshoots to −148°, 347° total.
- **Ruled out: the roll-reference gate.** `BoosterRollRangeDeg` already frees/holds roll by attitude
  error, and its history proves it useless here: 45° → 367°, 130° → 347°, both ~350°. H1 refuted; global
  HoldRoll worse (675→852).
- **SHIPPED (grounded, safe, reversible): `Attitude.CoastMaxRateDps` 4.0 → 3.0, now [Tunable].** F9I's
  coast PEAKS at 2.9°/s and rolls only 102°; we capped at 4.0, overshot to ~5, rolled 347°. A gentler
  slew = smaller pitch/yaw RCS torque = less cross-coupling into the weak roll axis. Timing is safe (a
  ~60 s reorientation inside a 106 s coast; it began 33 s in with 74 s left). Tunable so it can be swept
  in flight; raise it if the reorient stops finishing before the entry burn.
- **The full cure (deferred, NOT shipped): a coordinated single-axis slew** in `DriveInner` so the 180°
  turn induces no roll. That rewrites the shared attitude controller EVERY vehicle uses, and a frame bug
  there would tumble the craft — too dangerous to bundle with the launch that must validate the de-orbit
  fix. Its own isolated pass, designed not guessed. Recorded in `BoosterRecovery.cs` (Aim ~1595),
  `Attitude.cs` (CoastMaxRateDps doc), and the `BoosterRollRangeDeg` doc.

## 5. SECOND FLIGHT (flight_0818_154218) — what it taught, and the fixes on top
Flew 8f22e116. Results and the fixes they drove (all in DLL **72340895**, installed, UNFLOWN):

- **De-orbit landed 1103 km off — ROOT-CAUSED and FIXED.** The adaptive search PREDICTED 2.0 km but the
  capsule flew 1103 km off (log: `adaptive de-orbit ignition: a burn to -31.8 km would land 2.0 km`,
  then `known-bc(440) ... = 1103.4 km from target`). Cause: `IgnitionMissAt` scored the miss in the body
  frame AS OF NOW, ignoring the ~104° Kerbin turns across the 6262 s ignition lead (6262/21600·360 =
  104° = ~1090 km). FIX: wind the impact lon back by `(ut-searchNow)*360/rotationPeriod`, exactly as
  `MissAt` already does for cross-track (`DeorbitOps.cs` IgnitionMissAt). New rotating-body headless test
  in `DeorbitPointTest` (16 checks) that FAILS without the wind-back — the old test used a non-rotating
  body, which is why it missed this. **Watch next flight:** the `a burn to X km would land Y km` line
  should now agree with the actual splashdown, both small.
- **Roll-lock mitigation (CoastMaxRateDps 3.0) FAILED** - coast still rolled 358° (was 347). Slew RATE
  was never the lever. Restored to 4.0. Replaced with the real mechanism-based fix:
- **Booster ROLL LOCK (new user rule): SHIPPED, needs verification.** Rule: booster roll stays locked to
  launch through every maneuver. MEASURED cause: the roll is induced ONLY by SIMULTANEOUS pitch+yaw slew
  (pure-yaw slew rolls ~0; the instant the 180° pitch flip lands on top of the standing ~78° yaw error,
  roll explodes). FIX: `AttitudeController.SequenceLargeSlews` (booster, coast/entry-burn/descent) - while
  a reorientation is large, slew only the LARGER of pitch/yaw and HOLD the other, so each stays a
  single-axis rotation (which induces no roll). `[Tunable] SlewSequenceGateDeg = 20°`. Cannot tumble (it
  still drives to target, one axis first) - chosen over the coordinated-slew rewrite, which needs
  untestable quaternion frame math in the shared controller. **Watch:** coast roll should drop toward
  F9I's 102°, and the reorientation must still finish before the entry burn.
- **Rendezvous - the "longer rendezvous" FIXED (targeted, safe).** It reached the 10 km gate but at
  ~20 m/s relative (a fast crossing the phasing left); the direct approach got pushed back out of the
  gate and the leg dispatch fell into PHASING again - a whole new lap out to 22 km. FIX: a `reachedGate`
  latch (StationApproach.cs) - once the range first reaches the direct-approach gate, NO new phasing
  laps; if thrown back out, ride the existing intercept (match velocity) instead. Low-risk: intercept
  searches a wide window and recovers; reset per engagement. Does NOT redesign the deeper issue (the
  phasing leaves a fast crossing / the direct approach on distance alone) - that leg-gating rebuild is
  still its own careful pass, but this stops the wasteful re-phasing the crew saw.
