Decided

Settled 2026-09-02 — these are final

4 clusters The four things that had no real evidence and no existing asset. Each was resolved with the owner rather than invented.

Menu & Reference Content

reconstruct

Both resolved as reconstruct-from-function — see the Design set above. Real content, our layout, marked as a reconstruction. They stop being dark.

Console lighting

no red

Buttons light bright when active, armed or fired — crew-visible, matching the real console. No red state: nothing public shows a red button, so the old red-on-refusal is removed. Every mechanical press gets an audible click. A refused press is click, no light, no action.

Inferred controls

inert

Power, strings, resets, the fire/leak responses and the confirmed entry commands are real display state. SWAP 1/2/3 and the inferred entry-mode toggles stay inert — they click and do nothing — until a real console-procedure source verifies what they do. Not guessing is the decision.

Suit-leak fail branch

keep

Kept, and marked as a reconstruction. A leak check must have a fail path; no single photo captures below the fold. Absence in a photo is not absence — so the branch stays, honestly labelled.

What changed on 2026-09-02: the “genuinely dark” column is empty. Menu and Reference Content were resolved by owner decision rather than left blank; Ascent turned out to be data-known with only its layout inferred; and the old Proximity/ISS-plot gap is answered by the rendezvous ellipse and circular nav plot. A pass through the NASA JSC crew-training photo series — better-lit than any Discovery frame — added three real screen looks: the Prop thruster schematic, the systems tree, and the circular nav plot.

The honest limits. Those JSC frames are shot upward at the crew, so layouts are captured and exact on-screen text is not — every page built from them is layout-real, labels-reconstructed, and labelled that way. The physical console band is still not label-legible in any public image, so the emergency-plate labels remain unverified. What is left is label-level and gated on non-public material; it is carried as a caveat, not a blocker.
