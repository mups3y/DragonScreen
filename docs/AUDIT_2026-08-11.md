# DragonScreen — full-mod audit, 2026-08-11

Written after the crew abandoned the 10:37 flight. 24 152 lines across 84 files.

## Method and coverage

Every file was read. The flight-software path was read line by line as logic (comments stripped, so
the ~55% of this codebase that is commentary did not hide the code). The drawing layer was read the
same way and additionally machine-checked for the defect classes that matter to it. On top of the
reading, the whole tree was swept mechanically for: statics no lifecycle method clears, initialisation
made unreachable by an early `return`, duplicated constants, empty catches, unbounded loops, stale
"NOT WIRED" claims, public members with no caller, control rects that fall outside the drawable area,
and panel commands with no handler.

Evidence: `KSP.log` (396 DragonScreen lines), `flight_0811_103730.csv` (24 129 rows × 145 columns),
one cockpit photograph.

**Two things I checked and did not report**, because reading further proved the first impression
wrong — worth recording so nobody re-finds them:
* The sequence times and BODY row look misaligned in the photograph. `StepColumn:772-774` and
  `SideRow:787-788` each draw label and value at the **same `y`, same font**. It is perspective from
  an angled screen. CLAUDE.md rule 0.
* `Approach.BandMidV = 5.0` contradicts its own comment ("300 m → 3 m/s"). F9I's `stBandMidV` **is 5**.
  Our constants are right; we faithfully copied F9I's inaccurate comment. Do not "fix" the code.

## Findings

### CRITICAL — these stopped the flight

| # | fault |
|---|---|
| **C1** | **The rendezvous can never complete — two legs contradict each other.** `StationApproach:153` recomputes `Leg` **statelessly every tick**. Phasing's whole purpose is to *deliberately* change our SMA; `LegFor:105` returns `MatchOrbit` whenever SMA differs by ≥500 m. So the tick after the phasing entry burn, `Leg` flips to `MatchOrbit`, `FlyPhasing` is never re-entered, and `phaseReturnUt` (`:351`) — the circularisation that *exits* the phasing orbit — is unreachable. **The vehicle enters a phasing orbit and never leaves it.** |
| **C2** | **`OrbitMatch` measures one error and corrects another.** `Phasing.cs:196` `Needed()` tests \|ourSma−stationSma\|; `CirculariseAtApoapsisDv` drives SMA to *our own apoapsis radius*. The test can never go false. It also **diverges** on burn residue (0.04–0.05 m/s each, three "burned past the node"). Measured: 28 attempts, 593 → 12 986 m, **11.7 h of warp in a 20-minute session**. |
| **C3** | **No mission phase has a bound** — no attempt counter, no Δv budget, no convergence test, no give-up, anywhere in the ladder. C1/C2 are the bugs; C3 is why they ran for hours instead of failing in two minutes. |
| **C4** | **The launch-window hold was pasted into `Engage`, not `Tick`** (`AutoPilot:128-149`). Its `return` skips **ten** statements including **`PrepareForSeparation`** (→ booster unloads and is lost, 0 "physics range raised" lines), `phaseStartedAt` (→ every phase timeout uses the *previous* flight's clock) and **`s2Separated`** (→ **a second flight in one session never separates the S2**). |
| **C5** | **`NodeExecutor` is a global with no owner.** `PhaseDownOps` logged `LOWERING PERIAPSIS - Holding orbit match` — it found *StationApproach's* burn and waited forever for one it never planned. |
| **C6** | **Nothing arbitrates between subsystems.** `StationApproach` and `DeorbitOps` were both engaged, both driving `AttitudeController.Ascent`. `FlightDriver` has not one `Engaged` cross-check. |
| **C7** | **No abort.** `DeorbitOps.Engage` refuses when engaged; `FlightCommands:148` maps the button to `Engage()`, not `Toggle()`. The crew pressed DEORBIT NOW to escape and got *"Press ignored."* |
| **C8** | **The direct approach is locked out at exactly its design range.** `StationApproach:153` excludes `ApproachLeg.MatchOrbit` — the leg the flight was in at 4.4 km. The comment three lines above says F9I gates it *"at stDirectMax and nowhere else"*. |
| **C9** | **Warp is unbounded** — 11.7 h in ~25-min blocks at up to 100×. |

## The plan

Ordered so the cheap certain fixes are proved by a flight *before* the structural rework.

### Step 1 — Undo today's damage · C4, C7, H2, H3, H4 · ~1 session
1. Pad hold moves from `Engage` into `Tick`, after the packed check; `Engage` falls through to
   `PrepareForSeparation` unconditionally.
2. `liftoffUt` set when the vehicle actually leaves the pad.
3. `DeorbitNow` → `Toggle()`; a second press cancels and says so.
4. Only `FlightDriver` owns the recorder's lifetime.
5. `MissionRect` measured up from `ChromeBar.TopY`; **the rect sweep written during this audit
   becomes a permanent test** over every control at every screen size.

### Step 2 — Make the rendezvous terminate · C1, C2, C3, C8, C9, M4
1. **Give the ladder memory.** A leg that has committed to a manoeuvre owns the vehicle until it
   completes or is abandoned; `LegFor` becomes a *proposal* that only applies when no leg is
   mid-manoeuvre. This alone fixes C1.
2. `OrbitMatch` corrects the error it measures — target the **station's SMA** (two-burn Hohmann);
   `Needed` false when one burn cannot close it. Phasing exits at the apsis it entered from.
3. Hard budget on the whole rendezvous: attempts **and** total Δv. Exceeded → stop, hold, report.
4. Direct-approach gate: **distance and nothing else.**
5. One warp capped at a single orbital period; never warp inside the direct gate.
6. Headless test driven from **this flight's geometry** (4.4 km, 750 m SMA error), asserting arrival
   in a bounded number of burns. It fails today.

### Step 3 — Fly it
Launch → insertion → booster landing. Read `b_phase`, `b_predMissKm`, `m_rndz`. §4 is fully ported,
audited twice, and **has still never landed.** This step also settles H6, whose termination test has
never once fired.

### Step 4 — One owner per actuator · C5, C6
1. `NodeExecutor.Begin` takes an owner token; `Active`/`Phase`/`Note` readable only by the owner.
2. A single mission-phase arbiter — ascent, rendezvous, docking, undock, return mutually exclusive.
3. Tests: two phases cannot engage; a phase never observes a burn it did not plan.

### Step 5 — Correctness debt · H1, H5, H7, M1–M8, L1–L6
Packed-check before `Guide`; collapse the duplicated landing orbit, `0.333` and the three drogue
altitudes to one home each; delete `StartDeorbit` and point WATER DEORBIT at `DeorbitOps` with a
water target; fix the stale NOT-WIRED block; exercise `PredictedMiss`'s sign in a test; annotate the
bare catch; make `SwapString` return false; per-field validity in `VesselData`; quieten L1/L2.

## The pattern worth naming

Of the nine critical faults, **four (C4, C7, C8 and H4's broken test) were introduced today** — each
a change that was correct in isolation and never run against the sequence it sat in. Two more (C1,
C2) are older and were never exercised because the rendezvous had no caller until today.

`docs/PORT_PLAN.md` rule 0 says a phase with no caller is not ported. It needs a companion:

> **A phase that has not been flown is not finished, and a fix that has not been flown is not a fix.
> One launch-to-insertion run costs less than any single fault above.**
