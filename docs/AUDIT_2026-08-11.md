Written after the crew abandoned the 10:37 flight. 24 152 lines, 84 files.

## Coverage

**All 84 files, 24 152 lines, read.** An earlier draft claimed this before it was true; this section
is now accurate and the second pass is what made it so.

The reading was done on the code with block comments stripped, so the ~55% of this codebase that is
commentary could not hide a statement — then the comments were read separately, which is how the
stale "NOT WIRED" block (M1) and the inherited-wrong band comment were found.

On top of the reading, the whole tree was swept mechanically for: statics no lifecycle method clears,
initialisation made unreachable by an early `return`, duplicated constants, empty catches, unbounded

## Three first impressions I discarded on further reading

Recorded because acting on any of them would have broken working code.

1. **"RCS torque is under-reported 12×."** I committed that this morning off a 4.1 kN·m reading. It is
   wrong — see **C10**: RCS is *switched off* during the flip, so 4.1 (reaction wheels alone) was the
   correct reading. The MechJeb-derived RCS torque port is still right in itself but fixes nothing
   here. The residual gap between reported torque and achieved angular acceleration is **unexplained**
   and must be measured, not reasoned about.
2. **The cockpit photo's text misalignment.** `StepColumn:772-774` and `SideRow:787-788` draw label and
   value at the **same `y`, same font**. Perspective from an angled screen. CLAUDE.md rule 0.
3. **`Approach.BandMidV = 5.0` contradicting its comment ("300 m → 3 m/s").** F9I's `stBandMidV` **is
   5**. Our constant is right; we copied F9I's inaccurate comment.

## Findings

### CRITICAL

| # | fault |
|---|---|
| **C1** | **The rendezvous can never complete.** `StationApproach:153` recomputes `Leg` **statelessly every tick**. Phasing's purpose is to *deliberately* change SMA; `LegFor:105` returns `MatchOrbit` whenever SMA differs by ≥500 m. So the tick after the phasing entry burn, `FlyPhasing` is never re-entered and `phaseReturnUt` (`:351`) — the circularisation that *exits* the phasing orbit — is unreachable. **The vehicle enters a phasing orbit and never leaves it.** |
| **C2** | **`OrbitMatch` measures one error and corrects another** (`Phasing.cs:196`): `Needed()` tests \|ourSma−stationSma\|, the burn drives SMA to *our own apoapsis radius*. Cannot go false; **diverges** on burn residue. 28 attempts, 593 → 12 986 m, **11.7 h of warp**. |
| **C3** | **No mission phase has a bound** — no attempt counter, no Δv budget, no convergence test, no give-up, anywhere in the ladder. |
| **C4** | **The launch-window hold was pasted into `Engage`, not `Tick`** (`AutoPilot:128-149`). Its `return` skips **ten** statements, including **`PrepareForSeparation`** (→ booster unloads and is lost), `phaseStartedAt` (→ phase timeouts run on the *previous flight's* clock) and **`s2Separated`** (→ **a second flight in one session never separates the S2**). |
| **C5** | **`NodeExecutor` is a global with no owner.** `PhaseDownOps` logged `LOWERING PERIAPSIS - Holding orbit match` — it adopted StationApproach's burn and waited forever for one it never planned. |
| **C6** | **Nothing arbitrates between subsystems.** `StationApproach` and `DeorbitOps` were both engaged, both driving `AttitudeController.Ascent`. |
| **C7** | **No abort.** `FlightCommands:148` maps DEORBIT NOW to `Engage()`, not `Toggle()`; `Engage` refuses when engaged. The crew's escape attempt got *"Press ignored."* |
| **C8** | **The direct approach is locked out at its design range** — `StationApproach:153` excludes `MatchOrbit`, the leg the flight was in at 4.4 km. |
| **C9** | **Warp is unbounded** — 11.7 h in ~25-min blocks at up to 100×. |
| **C10** | **RCS is forced OFF during the flip and the boostback.** `LandingCommand.Rcs` is set true only at `Landing:740` (Coast-descending) and false at `:790`; every other phase leaves the struct default **false**, and `BoosterRecovery:435` actively `SetGroup(RCS,false)`. So in Flip/BoostbackKill/Boostback: throttle 0 → no gimbal torque, RCS off → no RCS torque, **reaction wheels only**. F9I's `Flip1` does `rcs on` and says why — *"the one moment the booster is asked to rotate hard, on a nearly empty stage, with no aerodynamic authority."* This is why the flip took 152 s. |
| **C11** | **`pure/Entry.cs` is entirely dead.** `Entry.Guide`, `EntryBand`, `AngleFor`, `DrogueAltitude` have **no caller**. The capsule's attitude-band schedule (0 / 15 / 8.25 / 1.95° by altitude) — the law carrying the CargoDragon_012 lesson, an entire entry flown 134.9° off the nose — is never applied. PORT_PLAN E7 claims it "composes with the new range controller". It composes with nothing. Fifth written-and-unreachable item. |

## The plan

### Step 1 — Undo today's damage · C4, C7, H1, H7, H8
Pad hold into `Tick`; `Engage` always reaches `PrepareForSeparation`. `liftoffUt` set at actual
liftoff. `DeorbitNow` → `Toggle()`. Recorder owned only by `FlightDriver`. `MissionRect` measured up
from `ChromeBar.TopY`, and **the rect sweep written during this audit becomes a permanent test.**

### Step 2 — Give the booster its authority back · C10, H2, H3, H5
`LandingCommand.Rcs = true` for Flip, BoostbackKill and Boostback. Implement the roll-settle gate the
three constants already describe. Delete the invented `max()` in `GuidanceAoaDeg`. Put a test on
`PredictedMiss`'s sign so H5 cannot stay unexercised.

### Step 3 — Make the rendezvous terminate · C1, C2, C3, C8, C9, H4, M4
Give the ladder **memory**: a leg that has committed owns the vehicle until its manoeuvre completes;
`LegFor` becomes a proposal. `OrbitMatch` targets the **station's SMA**. Hard attempt + Δv budget on
the whole rendezvous. Direct-approach gate on **distance only**. One warp capped at one period. Two
speed ladders collapse to one. Headless test from **this flight's geometry** (4.4 km, 750 m SMA
error), asserting arrival in a bounded number of burns — it fails today.

### Step 4 — Fly it
Launch → insertion → booster landing. §4 is fully ported, audited twice, and **has never landed.**

### Step 5 — One owner per actuator · C5, C6
`NodeExecutor.Begin` takes an owner token. One mission-phase arbiter; phases mutually exclusive.

### Step 6 — Wire or delete · C11, H9, H10, M1–M3
Wire `pure/Entry.cs` into `EntryOps` or delete it and say so. Collapse the duplicated landing orbit,
`0.333` and the three drogue altitudes. Retire `StartDeorbit`. Fix the stale NOT-WIRED block.

### Step 7 — Remainder
M5–M9, L1–L7, and a statement-by-statement read of the 22 drawing files listed under coverage.

## The pattern

Of eleven critical faults, **four were introduced today** (C4, C7, C8, and H1's broken test) and
**five are "written, tested, and connected to nothing"** (C11 plus the four fixed this morning). Two
more (C1, C2) were never exercised because the rendezvous had no caller until today.

`docs/PORT_PLAN.md` rule 0 says a phase with no caller is not ported. Its companion:

> **A phase that has not been flown is not finished, and a fix that has not been flown is not a fix.**

And a third, earned by C10 and the RCS-torque claim I got wrong:

> **A number that disagrees with the vehicle's behaviour is a measurement to take, not a conclusion
> to draw.**
